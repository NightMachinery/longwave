# Cooperative

Cooperative mode removes the two-team competition and turns the room into a shared puzzle.

## Who it is for

- Works with 2 or more players
- No formal LEFT/RIGHT team split during play

## Round flow

1. A clue-giver is selected for the round.
2. That player sees the spectrum card and hidden target.
3. The clue-giver submits one clue.
4. Everyone else collaborates on one shared guess.
5. Score is revealed.
6. Another player can draw the next card.

## Scoring

- The room has one shared cooperative score.
- A perfect hit counts as 3 points instead of 4.
- Perfect hits also add a bonus round.
- The game ends after the base round count plus any earned bonus rounds.

## Current implementation notes

- No counter-guess phase.
- No team assignment requirement.
- If future role controls are added here, they should probably be phrased as "who may clue" or "who may submit the room's guess" rather than team representatives.
