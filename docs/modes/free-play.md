# Free Play

Free Play keeps the hidden-target / clue / guess structure but strips out competitive scoring.

## Who it is for

- Works with 2 or more players
- Best when the group mainly wants discussion rather than a win condition

## Round flow

1. A clue-giver is selected for the round.
2. That player sees the spectrum card and hidden target.
3. The clue-giver submits one clue.
4. The room makes one shared guess.
5. The answer is revealed.
6. Another player can draw the next card.

## Scoring

- No running score.
- No winner.
- The main output is the reveal and discussion.

## Current implementation notes

- No teams.
- No counter-guess phase.
- This mode is the loosest fit for moderator / representative restrictions unless you specifically want them to apply outside team play.
